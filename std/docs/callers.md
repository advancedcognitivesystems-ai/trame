# Callers

*Who may call each entry point, from where, and the records it reaches that belong to someone, as of 28 November 2026. Its audience says who may call it; its trigger says where from. Records that belong to a person or a tenant ask an audience that carries that identity, or one that reaches every owner's records; until identification comes (C7), nothing checks who calls.*
